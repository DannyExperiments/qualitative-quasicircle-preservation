# Scope

The theorem concerns forward preservation by homeomorphisms of closed oriented surfaces, with quasiconformality allowed to reverse orientation. It includes the sphere under the all-quasicircle hypothesis and all positive genera under the essential-quasicircle hypothesis. The graph corollary uses the source's reconstruction theorem in genus at least two. The proof does not claim a quantitative bound in terms of a specified set of curve constants or a characterization for punctured surfaces. The examples illustrate the turning estimates.
